## Conventions

- **Rewriteable.** Every page under `wiki/` may be regenerated at any time. Do not put
  anything here you cannot afford to lose, except inside the editable region below.
- **The editable region.** Each entity and synthesis page carries a `## Related` section
  bracketed by HTML marker comments. Text inside the markers survives regeneration; text
  outside is replaced. If the markers are damaged, the page is skipped and reported
  rather than overwritten.
- **Every claim cites.** A generated line that states a fact links to the raw file or
  vault note it came from with a wikilink that resolves. The synthesize and research
  skills refuse to write a page whose citations do not resolve.
- **Rebuildable.** Deleting `wiki/` and re-running materialize, graduate, and synthesize
  reproduces it. This holds only while `raw/` stays append-only; see `raw/README.md`.
- **Slugs.** Entity and concept filenames are `Title-Case-With-Hyphens.md`; thread slugs
  are `lowercase-with-hyphens` and match the `#thread/<slug>` tag exactly.
